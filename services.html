Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) Services

Advanced Underground Detection and Mapping Solutions

TKM Surveying Solutions and Consulting offers Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) services to support the detection, investigation and mapping of subsurface features without the need for extensive excavation.

Using non-invasive geophysical technology, GPR surveys help identify and locate underground utilities, buried infrastructure and other subsurface anomalies. These services assist engineers, contractors, developers and project managers in making informed decisions, improving site safety and reducing the risk of costly damage during excavation and construction.

Our GPR Applications Include:

Underground Utility Detection

Locating buried water pipes, electrical cables, telecommunications lines and other underground services where detectable.

Subsurface Mapping

Investigating buried structures, obstructions and subsurface anomalies.

Construction and Engineering Support

Providing subsurface information for infrastructure development, site investigations and construction planning.

Concrete Scanning

Identifying embedded services, reinforcement and other detectable features within concrete structures, subject to suitable equipment and survey conditions.

Pre-Excavation Investigations

Supporting safer excavation, trenching, drilling and foundation works.

Surveying and Mapping Integration

Integrating GPR findings with topographical survey data and CAD deliverables to provide a more comprehensive understanding of site conditions.

Our Commitment

At TKMCS Group, we aim to deliver reliable subsurface investigation solutions that complement our surveying services. By combining modern detection technology with professional surveying and mapping expertise, we help clients improve project planning, minimise excavation risks and make better-informed engineering decisions.

GPR results are dependent on ground conditions, material composition, target depth, target size and equipment capabilities. GPR findings should be interpreted alongside other available utility records and detection methods where appropriate.
